# Module: contract first-pass review

**Recommended default: on.** Cheap first-pass read, meaningful risk
reduction — catches the obvious before it reaches (paid) counsel.

## What a 30-year contracts read looks for first

Before reading a contract clause-by-clause, an experienced reviewer scans
for the handful of terms that determine how much the rest matters:

- **Termination and renewal mechanics** — silent auto-renewal, asymmetric
  termination rights, notice periods that don't match the relationship's
  real cadence.
- **Liability and indemnity shape** — uncapped liability, one-sided
  indemnification, insurance requirements disproportionate to the deal
  size.
- **Who owns what gets created** — IP assignment terms, especially in
  services/dev work, are the single most commonly mis-negotiated term in
  SME contracts.
- **Governing law/jurisdiction** — a contract governed by unfamiliar law
  for no clear commercial reason is itself a flag.
- **Payment terms vs. market norm** — 90-day payment terms from a small
  supplier to a large customer is a cash flow risk hiding in a "standard"
  contract.

## Process

1. Use `../templates/contract-review-checklist-template.md` for every
   contract reviewed.
2. Read the five areas above first — they determine how carefully the rest
   needs reading.
3. Recommend one of: proceed, needs counsel review (state why), needs
   renegotiation (state which terms) — never a bare "looks fine."

## Where humans add value

Signing, negotiating, and any judgment call where the flagged risk is
"technically concerning but commercially acceptable given the
relationship" — that tradeoff needs the founder's actual risk tolerance
and relationship context, not this module's.

## Efficiency note

Read the contract once; don't re-read it for each checklist item
separately — score all flags in a single pass.
