# Expert questions — Finance

The questions a 30-year controller/CFO asks that a template doesn't force.
Agents: raise the relevant question when its trigger appears — don't recite
the whole list in every report. Humans: use these in accountant meetings
and when reading any finance output.

## Reading a P&L

- Is revenue growing because of price, volume, or mix? Each has a
  completely different meaning for next year.
- What's in "other" or "miscellaneous"? The most interesting number is
  usually the one nobody categorized.
- Which costs are actually fixed vs. labeled fixed? (Rent is fixed;
  "salaries" often isn't as fixed as the owner believes, and utilities
  aren't as variable.)
- If this month looks good: what one-off made it look good? If bad: what
  one-off is being blamed, and would the month still look bad without it?
- Are margins moving because of the business, or because of how things are
  being booked? (Reclassifications quietly manufacture trends.)

## Reading a balance sheet

- Who actually owes us this receivables number — and would they agree they
  owe it? (Disputed AR hides inside "current.")
- What's the inventory really worth if we had to sell it in 90 days?
- Which liabilities aren't on here? (Leases, guarantees given, accrued
  vacation days, the owner's informal promises to staff or customers.)
- Is the owner's private wallet entangled with the company's? (Current
  account with the owner — `rekening-courant` — direction and trend.)

## Cash

- When exactly does cash bottom out in the next 13 weeks — not "is there
  enough this month"?
- Which customer payment, if it slipped 30 days, would cause real trouble?
- Are we financing our customers (long payment terms given) with our
  suppliers' patience (terms taken)? How long does that stay stable?

## When a number smells wrong

- Is this number too smooth? Real businesses are lumpy; smooth series are
  usually managed series.
- Would this number survive the owner explaining it to a skeptical buyer?
- What would the bookkeeper say caused it — and does that story match what
  operations says happened that month?

## For the accountant meeting (per opco)

- What did you correct most often in this company's books last year?
- Where do you disagree with how the previous accountant treated things?
- What's the one tax/structuring item you'd fix if it were your company?
