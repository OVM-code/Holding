# Module: content & brand calendar

**Recommended default: off** — turn on once an opco has an active marketing
motion (regular content, social posting, seasonal campaigns) worth
tracking; a dormant calendar is pure overhead.

## What a 30-year marketing operator tracks here

- **Consistency over volume.** A calendar's real value isn't scheduling —
  it's catching drift: has the brand voice shifted between posts, is one
  channel getting all the attention while another goes silent, is there a
  gap around a known seasonal moment that matters for this business.
- **The 80/20 of content types.** Most SME marketing calendars overweight
  novel content and underweight the small set of proven formats
  (testimonials, before/after, FAQ-answering) that reliably perform —
  tracking what's been tried and what worked is more valuable than
  planning what's next in isolation.

## Process

1. Maintain `../templates/marketing-calendar-template.csv`.
2. On each check, flag: channels with no activity logged in over 30 days,
   inconsistency against any documented brand guidelines, upcoming known
   seasonal/business moments with nothing planned.
3. Draft content *within* the opco's established voice when asked — never
   invent a new voice or positioning unprompted.

## Where humans add value

Deciding what to actually say, approving anything before it's published,
and owning the channels that are fundamentally relational (in-person,
referral, direct outreach) rather than calendar-schedulable.

## Efficiency note

Update the calendar incrementally as activity is reported — don't
regenerate the full calendar view from scratch each check.
