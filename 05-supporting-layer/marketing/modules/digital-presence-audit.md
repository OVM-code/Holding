# Module: digital presence audit

**Recommended default: off**, but cheap to run periodically (e.g. quarterly)
even for opcos with low marketing activity — a stale or broken digital
presence is a silent tax on every other marketing effort.

## What a 30-year practitioner checks first

- **The basics that get neglected**: is the Google Business Profile
  current (hours, address, phone), are contact details consistent across
  every listing, does the website work on mobile, is there an obvious
  broken link or outdated page a first-time visitor would hit.
- **Review signal**: review volume, recency, and whether negative reviews
  have any response — for most local Belgian SMEs, this matters more to
  new-customer conversion than SEO technicalities.
- **Basic SEO hygiene, not deep SEO strategy**: page titles/meta
  descriptions present and sensible, the business's actual name/location
  appearing clearly, no obvious technical breakage (broken sitemap, missing
  key pages) — a first-pass hygiene check, not a full SEO audit.
- **Consistency across surfaces**: same name, address, phone, and brand
  voice across website, social profiles, and listings — inconsistency
  quietly erodes both trust and local search ranking.

## Process

1. Use `../templates/digital-presence-checklist-template.md`.
2. Check what's publicly accessible (website, Google Business Profile,
   main social channels) — this module works from what's visible, not
   privileged analytics access unless provided.
3. Flag issues by severity: broken/wrong (fix now) vs. stale/suboptimal
   (worth improving) vs. fine.

## Where humans add value

Actually making the fixes (website changes, responding to reviews,
updating listings), and any decision about SEO/marketing investment level
— this module surfaces the audit, it doesn't execute the fix.

## Efficiency note

This is a periodic-audit module by design — running it more than quarterly
for a low-activity opco burns allowance on a check that won't have changed.
