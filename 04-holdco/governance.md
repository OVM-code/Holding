# Governance (pre-first-deal)

Lightweight by design — this is a solo operation right now. Revisit as the
first opco is acquired and/or additional people join.

## Decision rights

- All deal decisions (screen, diagnose, structure, exercise option) are made
  by the founder. Agents draft and recommend; they do not decide.
- A `demand-decay` verdict or a hard disqualifier from
  `00-thesis/investment-thesis.md` is a strong default to walk away — 
  overriding it requires writing down the specific reason in the diagnosis
  file, not just proceeding silently.

## Cadence

- No fixed board/reporting cadence yet — there's nothing to report on.
  Add a section here once the first service-phase engagement starts,
  covering: engagement health checks, financial reporting cadence from the
  opco, and option-decision countdown tracking.

## When this needs to grow

Add real governance structure (board, advisor input, written decision
criteria beyond this repo) once either:

- The first acquisition closes, or
- A second person (employee, partner, investor) is involved in decisions.
