## Research workflow for a board question

1. Restate the question in the book's vocabulary.
2. Locate which framework/chapter governs it.
3. Apply the book's procedure to the specifics given.
4. State plainly where the question falls outside the book's scope — and
   stop there rather than improvising in the book's voice.
