## Founder brief (plain language)

Written by the chair AFTER the synthesis below, for a smart reader with no
finance/strategy vocabulary. This is the board's actual deliverable; the
synthesis below it is the audit trail. Rules: open with a 3-5 sentence
verdict a busy person could read alone; no jargon (any technical term gets
one plain sentence of explanation where first used); every conclusion
gets a concrete "what this looks like for you" example; disagreement told
as a story — "most of the board says X, one voice warns Y" — with who,
why in one plain sentence each, and what evidence would settle it; ends
with a numbered action list (this week / this month / decide-by).
