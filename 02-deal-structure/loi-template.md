# Letter of Intent (LOI) — template

> ⚠ **Belgian counsel required.** This is a structural drafting aid, not a
> legal document. An LOI in Belgium can carry unintended binding effect
> (e.g. on exclusivity, break fees, or pre-contractual liability under
> `culpa in contrahendo`). Have a Belgian business lawyer review before
> sending to any owner, even in "non-binding" form.

**Between:** [Holding entity name] ("the Holding")
**And:** [Owner name / Company name] ("the Owner")

**Date:** [date]

## 1. Purpose

This letter sets out the Holding's intent to enter into a **service
engagement** with [Company name], with a possible subsequent acquisition,
on the terms summarized below. It is intended to guide detailed negotiation
of binding agreements and, except where explicitly stated, **is not
binding**.

## 2. Structure at a glance

1. **Service phase.** The Holding provides [services — e.g. back-office,
   financial reporting, ops support] to the Company in exchange for
   compensation combining cash and equity. See
   `service-agreement-template.md`.
2. **Option period.** During and/or after the service phase, the Holding
   holds an option to acquire [%] of the Company under the terms in
   `option-to-buy-template.md`.
3. **Decision point.** At [date / trigger condition], the Holding will
   decide whether to exercise the option.
