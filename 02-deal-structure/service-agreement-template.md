# Service Agreement (cash + equity) — template

> ⚠ **Belgian counsel required.** This template structures the commercial
> intent of a service-first engagement. It does not address Belgian
> corporate law formalities (e.g. capital contribution rules, `apport en
> nature`/`inbreng in natura` for equity issued as compensation), tax
> treatment of equity received for services, or employment/social-security
> implications if the Holding's principal works inside the Company. All of
> these need a Belgian lawyer and accountant before signing.

## 4. Access and information rights

The Provider needs sufficient access to financials, operations, and
management to actually deliver the services and to conduct the due diligence
this engagement doubles as. Specify:

- Access to accounting records / bank statements
- Attendance at management meetings
- Access to customer/supplier data (subject to confidentiality)

## 5. Confidentiality

Mutual. The Provider will accumulate deep knowledge of the Client's business
during this phase — this section should be tight and explicit about what
happens if no acquisition follows.

## 6. Relationship to the option to buy

This agreement and `option-to-buy-template.md` should be read together.
State explicitly whether performance under this agreement is a condition,
factor, or irrelevant to the option's exercise.
