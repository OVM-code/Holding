# Option to Buy — template

> ⚠ **Belgian counsel required.** Option agreements over shares in a Belgian
> company (BV/SRL or NV/SA) interact with statutory pre-emption rights,
> share transfer restrictions in the articles of association, and — if
> structured as an option over a *future* increase or transfer — potentially
> notarial formalities. Do not rely on this template's mechanics without a
> Belgian corporate lawyer confirming they're enforceable as drafted.

## 3. Exercise price / valuation mechanism

Pick one and specify — this is the most contested term in practice:

- [ ] Fixed price agreed now: [amount]
- [ ] Formula tied to financial performance (e.g. multiple of average EBITDA
  over the service phase): [formula]

⚠ Belgian counsel + accountant: confirm the valuation mechanism doesn't
create a disguised distribution or trigger unintended tax consequences,
especially since the Option Holder has been operating inside the business.
