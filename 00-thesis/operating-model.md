# Operating model

## The ambition

Build a small holding company that buys **sound but underperforming Belgian
SMEs** from owners ready to step away, runs the back office (finance, admin,
marketing, support) as one lean, AI-driven layer across the whole portfolio,
and puts real human effort into growing each business and creating good jobs.
Buy to fix and keep — not to strip and flip. Long-term value and employment.

Starting solo, growing into an employer that provides genuinely good work.
More people joining is expected and wanted — it is not a failure of the
"lean" idea.

## The model, and the contradiction it resolves

- **Leanness lives at the holding level, not the acquired-company level.**
  The holding stays tiny and agent-run. The businesses acquired come with
  staff, customers, and legacy mess — and get made leaner and stronger over
  time through the operating playbook, not by cutting people at the top.
- **Agentic overhead + human value creation.** Agents carry the portfolio's
  overhead — back-office, reporting, content, support, ops admin. People —
  including people employed well — do the operating work that actually grows
  each business. Agents are plumbing, not the identity of the company.
- This is a **people business with an agentic cost layer**, not a "lean
  magic" automated business. Value creation does not automate; only overhead
  does.

## Why this matters for how the repo is used

Any workflow or agent added to this repo should carry *overhead*: sourcing
research, screening, drafting, monitoring, reporting. If a proposed automation
would replace the human operating work inside a portfolio company — sales,
service delivery, management judgment, culture — it's out of scope. Flag it
and ask, don't build it silently.
